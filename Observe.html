[ 03 — Materiality & Practice ]

Uncompromising material integrity, faithfully sourced.

We reject superficial ornamentation in favor of honest tectonic expression. Every element is categorized by provenance, carbon index, and structural longevity.

[ 01 ] Rammed Earth Composite
Locally quarried sedimentary clay, stabilized with natural hydraulic lime and coarse aggregate. Zero synthetic polymers.
Classification: Structural Mass
[ 02 ] Untreated Japanese Hinoki
Selected architectural timber harvested from certified sustainable alpine forests in Nagano. Hand-planed finish.
Classification: Tectonic Envelope
[ 03 ] Raw Cast Bronze Patina
Sand-cast alloy components left unfinished to record tactile interaction and natural atmospheric oxidation over time.
Classification: Hardware & Detail
[ 04 ] Acoustic Wool Felt
Un-dyed organic merino wool pressed to 12mm density for high-performance sound absorption in interior wall planes.
Classification: Surface Acoustic
